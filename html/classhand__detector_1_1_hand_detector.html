Detailed Description

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker wrapper

Detects hands in images and returns 21 landmarks per hand.

Landmark Indices:
    0: Wrist
    1-4: Thumb (base to tip)
    5-8: Index finger
    9-12: Middle finger
    13-16: Ring finger
    17-20: Pinky
